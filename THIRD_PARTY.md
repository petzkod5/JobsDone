The bundled `Resources/Peon.gif` is the classic Warcraft III Orc Peon portrait, owned by Blizzard Entertainment. Downloaded from [Blizzard's Warcraft III unit portraits](https://classic.battle.net/war3/images/orc/units/portraits/peon.gif), as used on the official [Peon unit page](https://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/peon.shtml). The build packages the real portrait into the macOS app icon (`PeonPortrait.icns`), used by Setup Help and notification toasts, preserving its aspect ratio. The menu bar alone uses the user-selected white Profile B Peon glyph. Its editable path geometry is in `Resources/MenuBarOptions/options.json`. No ownership of the artwork is claimed.
